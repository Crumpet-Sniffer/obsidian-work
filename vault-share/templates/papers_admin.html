Add past papers and exam documents

Upload one .zip of everything from the exam board's assessment resources: past papers, mark schemes, the specification, examiner reports and guidance. Claude sorts it, splits each paper into questions with their mark schemes, and builds the paper guides. It takes a few minutes per paper, and you can leave the page. The questions appear in the question bank on the vault's Practice page.

How it works

Files can be PDF, Word or PowerPoint, in any folders, named however they came. Claude works out what each file is, matches each paper to its mark scheme, and reads each paper's qualification, paper number, month and year off its cover.

Each paper is split into its questions, each with its marks, type, topics and its part of the mark scheme; the other documents are read for their content areas, command words, marking guidance and what examiners found. Then the paper guides below are built from all of it.

Anything already in the hub is skipped, so you can upload a bigger zip later.

Or add files one at a time

A specification

A past paper and its mark scheme

The insert is a case study, source booklet or data sheet given out with the paper, if there was one.

    Paper guides

    For each paper of each qualification: the fields (specification areas) it can examine, with how many past questions each has, its format and command words, how marks are earned and what examiners say students get wrong. Built by Claude from the specification, past papers, examiner reports, commentaries and guidance, and used when it writes and marks questions for that paper.

    Papers by year

    Each exam year: every paper sat that year, the documents about it (examiner reports, commentaries, guidance), and what examiners said about the year as a whole. The examiners' notes are written by Claude from that year's documents when the guides are built.

    Course documents

    Specifications, and other documents not about one exam year.

    Clear data

    Clear the question hub

    Deletes every past paper and its questions, every exam document, the paper guides and the examiners' notes for each year from this vault's question hub. Questions Claude wrote, everyone's answers and history, flashcards and the exam coach are kept. This cannot be undone; upload the papers again to start over.

    Clear everyone's answer history

    Deletes every set of questions anyone has answered in this vault, with their answers, marks and feedback, so the history and the exam coach's results start again from nothing. The questions, flashcards, study guides, mind maps and chats are kept. This cannot be undone.
