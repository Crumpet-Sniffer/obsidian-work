Add past papers and exam documents

Put everything you downloaded from the exam board's assessment resources in one .zip (PDF, Word or PowerPoint, in any folders, named however they came): past papers, mark schemes, the course specification, examiner reports, answers and commentaries, and notes and guidance. Claude works out what each file is, matches each paper to its mark scheme, and reads each paper's qualification, paper number, month and year off its cover. Each paper is split into its questions, each with its marks, type, topics and its part of the mark scheme; the other documents are read for their content areas, command words, marking guidance and what examiners found. Then the paper guides below are built from all of it. Anything already in the hub is skipped, so you can upload a bigger zip later. It takes a few minutes per paper; you can leave the page.
