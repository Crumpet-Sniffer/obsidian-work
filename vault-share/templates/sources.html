Add Word, PowerPoint and PDF files, pictures, or a .zip of them (up to {{ max_file_mb }} MB each). Claude turns each into a note, files it in the right folder and links it to the topics and notes it relates to. Once the upload finishes you can close this page.

How it works

A zip is culled first: only its Word, PowerPoint, PDF and picture files are kept, from any subfolder, and everything else in it (videos, spreadsheets, system files, small icons) is left out and listed before anything is uploaded. A PDF's pictures, and the pages of a scanned PDF, are read like any other pictures.

Files added together are read first (including their pictures, by OCR or by Claude, as an admin chose), then Claude reads them all at once to plan their topics and folders, and then connects each one to the vault and to the rest of the batch.

The text of the files and a list of this vault's folders, note titles and topics are sent to Anthropic to decide where they go.
