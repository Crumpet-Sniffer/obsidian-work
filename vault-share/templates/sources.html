Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) files, up to {{ max_file_mb }} MB each, or a .zip of them: every Word and PowerPoint file in the zip is added, whatever subfolder it is in. Each one is converted to a note, filed in the folder where it belongs, and linked to the topics and notes it relates to. Files added together are read first (including the text in their pictures), then Claude reads them all at once to plan their topics and folders, and then connects each one to the vault and to the rest of the batch. The text of the files and a list of this vault's folders, note titles and topics are sent to Anthropic to decide where they go. The files are uploaded to the server and added there: once the upload has finished you can close this page, and come back to see how it went.
