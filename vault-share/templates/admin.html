Pictures in sources

OCR reads only the words in a picture, on this server, and the pictures are never sent anywhere. With Claude, each picture (scaled down to 1568 px) is sent to Anthropic. A picture model such as Claude Haiku 5.5 reads every picture first, several at a time, and passes the words it finds and a short description of each one on to the filing model: cheap, and fine for batches of any size. Without a picture model, the filing model is shown up to 100 pictures of each file itself, which costs more, and the batch plan sees only the text of the files.
